Tier classification rules
🎯 SOLID_LEAD_COMMERCIAL
Stage = evaluation/intent AND commercial keyword (pricing, contract, deployment…) AND 2+ products detected
🔭 TECH_PILOT_ENGAGED
Intent = compare / evaluate_specs / troubleshoot AND 1+ product detected
🌱 EARLY_EXPLORER
Default — awareness stage, learn_concept or find_resource, single product
⚠️ DEAD_END_SUPPORT
Intent = general AND no products AND 5+ consecutive low-score results

3+ similar queries within 3 minutes, no citation clicks. Second priority.

5 consecutive low-score results. Fires only if no other nudge shown.
